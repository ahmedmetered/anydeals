# Replies — handling responses

Classify first, draft second. The user forwards or pastes the reply; you return a draft and update the prospect's OUTBOX status.

## Intent classification

- INTERESTED: move toward a meeting within 1-2 replies. Do not oversell. Propose 2-3 specific times (or the booking link from persona.md if set).
- OBJECTION: acknowledge honestly, give ONE proof point from persona.md, ask one question. Never argue.
- QUESTION: answer it first, completely, then tie back to their situation in one line.
- WRONG PERSON: thank them, ask for the right name or a forward. Make it effortless.
- NOT INTERESTED: do not draft now. Mark closed for this campaign: ONE polite follow-up is allowed later (a light check-in, then stop). Opt-out and anger/legal remain permanent.
- UNSUBSCRIBE / OPT-OUT (any wording): never reply, never save it, mark permanently excluded in the OUTBOX and say so to the user. Non-negotiable.
- ANGRY / LEGAL / COMPLIANCE (lawyers, spam reports, harassment, GDPR mentions): never reply. Escalate verbatim to the user, mark the prospect closed.

## Drafting rules

- Match their energy: casual stays casual, formal stays formal.
- Short. They know who you are; skip re-introductions.
- If they asked a question, answer it in the first line. No dodging.
- One question or CTA per reply, maximum.
- No desperation tells: "just checking in", "just following up", "I'd love to", "great question", "I completely understand".
- Same banned-pattern rules as method.md apply (no em dashes, no exclamation marks, no bullets in the email body, no formatting).
- Never promise what is not in persona.md: no custom pricing, no invented customers, no dates or legal terms. If unknown: say you'll find out, then tell the user what's missing.

## Conversation stages (pace accordingly)

INITIAL → build rapport, ask about their situation, no pitch.
QUALIFYING → understand needs; budget/authority/timing asked naturally, never as a checklist.
PRESENTING → specifics from persona.md, examples that exist.
CLOSING → concrete meeting proposal, easy to say yes to.
